You are producing ONE character sheet image: a reference illustration that a developer will
hand-rebuild as flat inline SVG. It is a design document, not finished art.

GLOBAL STYLE (applies to every sheet)
- Heroic graphic cel: hard-edged, closed, flat colour regions with a consistent dark outline
  (about 2-3% of the figure height). Broad upper-left highlight shapes and lower-right shadow
  shapes, each a single closed region. NO gradients, NO soft shading, NO glow fields, NO
  blur, NO texture, NO vignette, NO painterly rendering.
- Every highlight and shadow is a flat, fully opaque, hard-edged closed shape with a crisp
  boundary. No semi-transparent overlays, no feathered or fading edges, no tonal blending.
  Count at most three tones per material (base, highlight, shadow).
- Use ONLY the hex colours listed under PALETTE. Do not introduce other colours.
- Background: one perfectly flat, uniform #FF00FF magenta fill across the whole canvas. Do NOT
  draw a checkerboard, transparency grid, shadow, floor, border or frame. No magenta may
  appear inside any figure.
- Silhouettes must stay readable at roughly 80 px tall: favour bold shapes, clear negative
  gaps between limbs and body, and few small details. Skip fine seams, buckles, fingers and
  stitching unless the brief asks for them.
- Landscape canvas, 3:2, high resolution. NO text, labels, captions,
  arrows, numbers, watermarks or signatures anywhere on the image.

SHEET LAYOUT (every sheet)
- TOP ROW: the canonical poses named in the brief, large, same scale, same ground line and
  same registration point so frames could be overlaid.
- MIDDLE ROW: construction parts drawn separately and fully visible (not overlapped), so each
  can be rebuilt as its own SVG group.
- BOTTOM ROW: only the proofs the brief names (for example a mirrored view), smaller.
- Generous even spacing; no element touches another or the canvas edge.
- Every pose faces the same direction unless the brief says otherwise.

FIDELITY RULES (follow literally)
- Draw exactly the number of poses, parts and proofs the brief lists, in the order listed,
  and nothing extra. Do not add features the brief does not name (for example a mouth, nose or
  accessory on a character whose brief omits it).
- When a brief gives a tilt, lean or pitch angle, apply it to the WHOLE figure by that angle.
- A pose described as "mirrored" must be an exact horizontal reflection of the named pose,
  not a new pose.
- Parts rows show each piece as it would be cut out for a rebuild: detached, unshaded by
  other parts, at the same scale as the canonical poses unless the brief says otherwise.
- Keep every pose's shared registration point (ground line or anchor) identical.
