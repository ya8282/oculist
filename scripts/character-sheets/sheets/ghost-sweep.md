SUBJECT: Ghost Sweep. A friendly-spooky classic bedsheet ghost that drifts across a web page
and sweeps past highlighted text. Reads as "ghost" instantly at small size.

PALETTE: sheet body #F2EEF8, sheet shadow #BDB4D6, deep shadow #8479A8, outline #2B2440,
eye and mouth #17121F, tiny cheek blush #F2A6C8 (optional, two small ovals).

CANONICAL POSES (top row, four, same scale):
1. Idle float: tall rounded dome head, sheet falling to a wavy three-scallop hem, two tall
   oval eyes, small round "o" mouth. No visible arms.
2. Drift left-lean: whole body tilted about 12 degrees, hem trailing in a long S-curve behind.
3. Wail: stretched taller, mouth a large tall oval, eyes drawn wide, hem flaring.
4. Fade-through: same as pose 1 but drawn as an outline-only silhouette with flat #BDB4D6
   fill and eyes kept solid, to show the "passing over text" state.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row, separate): bare sheet body with no face; the three hem
scallop variants (A, B, C) used to wobble the hem; eye pair; the three mouth shapes (round,
tall wail, flat smirk).

PROOFS (bottom row): a separate wavy trail ribbon, 2.5x the ghost width, tapering to a point,
flat #BDB4D6; and the ghost mirrored horizontally.

AVOID: arms, hands, chains, skulls, gore, dripping slime, realistic folds, transparency effects.
