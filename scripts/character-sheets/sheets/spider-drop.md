SUBJECT: Spider Drop. A cute-creepy spider lowering itself on a silk thread above a match,
bobbing in place. Chunky and readable, not realistic.

PALETTE: body #2A2233, body highlight #52456B, body shadow #14101A, outline #0B0810,
eyes #F2E94E, eye glint #FFFFFF, silk thread and web #E6E2EE, web shadow #A9A3BD,
small red hourglass mark #C93B2B.

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale, centred on one vertical thread):
1. Descend: body hanging head-down, eight legs bent up and out in a tight pose.
2. Hang: body upright-facing viewer, legs spread symmetric, mid-bob.
3. Recoil: legs pulled in tight against the body, as if startled.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): bare round abdomen with hourglass mark; smaller head with
six eyes in two rows (two large front eyes, four small); one leg drawn straight with three
joints, then the same leg bent; the thread with a small loop where it attaches to the body.

PROOFS (bottom row): a quarter-circle corner web (five radial lines, three connecting arcs)
sized to sit in a text-match corner, and the spider mirrored horizontally.

AVOID: hair or fur, fangs dripping, realistic anatomy, more or fewer than eight legs, gore.
