SUBJECT: Grave Hands. Zombie hands clawing up from a strip of ground, one reaching toward
a match. Funny-spooky, not gory.

PALETTE: skin #8FA878, skin highlight #B7CDA0, skin shadow #5C7552, outline #1B2616,
nail #D8CFA8, sleeve rag #4A3F58, sleeve shadow #2B2436, soil #4B3322, soil highlight #6E4C32,
soil shadow #2B1D12, small pebble #8C8070.

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale, each rising from its own soil mound):
1. Burst: a hand and wrist just breaking through the soil, fingers splayed.
2. Reach: forearm and ragged sleeve fully up and leaning right, fingers spread wide.
3. Grab: same forearm, fingers curled into a clutching claw.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): the hand with fingers spread, separate from the forearm;
the clutching claw; a ragged sleeve cuff with two torn points; the soil mound with three
lumps and a few pebbles; three loose clods that fly off when the hand bursts through.

PROOFS (bottom row): hands and soil mirrored, and a wider ground strip, 3x the mound width,
with a flat top edge that can tile.

AVOID: exposed bone, blood, gore, rot textures, grave markers or tombstones, extra fingers
(each hand has exactly five).
