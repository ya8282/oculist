SUBJECT: Cursed Rune. A glowing sigil circle that draws itself around a match, with a candle
flame at each of the four cardinal points.

PALETTE: sigil line #B58CFF, sigil bright #E3D2FF, sigil dark #5D3DA8, outline #1A1030,
candle wax #F2EEF8, wax shadow #BDB4D6, flame outer #E8A53A, flame mid #FFD45A,
flame core #FFF1A6, wick #17121F.

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale, each in a square cell):
1. Draw: the outer circle about 60 percent drawn (open arc), with the inner ring not started.
2. Complete: double ring with an inscribed five-point star, four simple rune glyphs between
   the rings, flat closed shapes, even line weight.
3. Pulse: the complete sigil drawn about 5 percent larger with the bright colour on the star
   and the dark colour on the outer ring.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): the outer ring alone; the inner ring alone; the star alone;
the four rune glyphs, each separate; a candle with three flame states (low, tall, flicker
left) at the same scale.

PROOFS (bottom row): the sigil shown as an inverted outline-only version (line only, no fill)
for a Lite Mode fallback, and one candle at 4x scale for registration.

AVOID: pentagram occult-religion imagery with a goat head, text in real languages, blood,
skulls, gore, glow fields or soft light.
