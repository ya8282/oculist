SUBJECT: Mummy Unwrap. A mummy's bandage strips unwinding across a match and falling away,
revealing it. Comic and friendly, not scary.

PALETTE: bandage #E8DCB8, bandage highlight #F8F0D6, bandage shadow #BFAE84,
bandage stain #A89870, outline #3A2E1C, eyes #FFD45A, eye pupil #17121F,
void between wraps #17121F.

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale):
1. Wrapped: a short upright mummy, diagonal criss-cross bandage bands over body and head,
   two eye gaps showing, arms folded forward.
2. Unwinding: one long bandage strip peeling off the shoulder in a loose spiral, the head
   half unwrapped.
3. Collapse: only a loose pile of crumpled strips with two eyes still visible in the pile.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): head with eye gaps; torso wraps; each arm separate; one single
bandage strip drawn straight (about 12:1), and the same strip curled in three flat phases
(loose spiral, S-curve, tight roll).

PROOFS (bottom row): a flat, tileable bandage-strip band, 6:1, with diagonal wrap lines and one
frayed end; the mummy mirrored.

AVOID: sarcophagi, hieroglyph text, gore, exposed skin or bone, realistic cloth folds.
