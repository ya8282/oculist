SUBJECT: Ouija Planchette. A wooden planchette that slides across the page, hovers, and
settles over a match, as if spelling it out. Reads as "spirit board" without a full board.

PALETTE: wood #A9743C, wood highlight #D49A5A, wood shadow #6B4423, outline #2A170A,
lens glass #CFE8E4, lens rim #E8C766, lens shadow #8FB5B0, brass pin foot #C9A24A,
engraved line #3C2410, candle glow accent #FFD45A (small flat tick marks only).

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale):
1. Rest: the classic teardrop or heart planchette, straight on, a round clear lens cut-out
   near the narrow tip, three small round feet visible as dark dots at the rim.
2. Slide: tilted about 10 degrees with two short flat motion ticks behind it.
3. Settle: same as rest but drawn very slightly bigger with four short "attention" ticks
   around the lens to show it has landed.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): the planchette body alone; the lens ring alone (one version
with solid glass, one with a plain hole); the three feet; the engraved crescent-and-star motif
for the broad face.

PROOFS (bottom row): the planchette mirrored; three engraved glyph shapes (crescent moon,
sun, and small star) as flat tick-mark symbols, each fitting inside a 1:1 box.

AVOID: full alphabet boards, hands, smoke, demons, skulls, realistic wood grain.
