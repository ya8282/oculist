silhouette. The match gets a thin rim of moonlight.

PALETTE: moon #F4E9B8, moon highlight #FFF8D8, moon shadow #D9C98A, crater #BDAE72,
outline none on the moon, wolf #14101F, wolf edge light #3A3260, eye #FFD45A,
cloud #4B4268, cloud shadow #2E2845, star #FFF1A6.

CANONICAL POSES (top row, three, same scale):
1. Rise: the moon as a circle about 35 percent hidden behind a flat horizon band of #14101F.
2. Full: the whole moon above the horizon with three flat crater circles.
3. Howl: the same full moon with the wolf standing on the horizon, head thrown back, mouth
   open, tail low, as a solid silhouette.

CONSTRUCTION PARTS (middle row): moon disc alone; the three craters; the wolf body, head and
tail as separate silhouettes; two thin elongated cloud strips; a four-point star and a
five-point star.

PROOFS (bottom row): a crescent-moon version for the reduced-motion state, and the wolf
silhouette mirrored.

AVOID: a face on the moon, bats crossing the moon (already in another effect), realistic fur,
glowing halos, gradients.
